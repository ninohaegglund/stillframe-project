Design a modern, minimalist website for an independent ambient music project called **Still Frame**.

### Concept

Still Frame is a small independent music project creating **dark, eerie, atmospheric and nostalgic ambient music inspired by video games, horror games and mysterious environments**.

The website should feel like entering a quiet, mysterious digital archive rather than a traditional musician/artist website.

The visual identity should be:

* Dark
* Atmospheric
* Minimal
* Cinematic
* Slightly eerie
* Nostalgic
* Sophisticated rather than overly “horror”
* Inspired by retro games, old CRT displays, abandoned places, forests, fog, night-time environments and surreal spaces

Avoid making it look like a generic Spotify clone or a typical musician portfolio.

### Color & typography

Use a very dark background, close to black/charcoal, with subtle off-white/gray text.

Use restrained accent colors such as muted gray, desaturated green, dark blue or a very subtle warm tone.

Typography should be clean and modern, with perhaps a slightly distinctive serif or monospace font used sparingly for atmospheric elements.

Keep contrast readable and the overall interface uncluttered.

### Homepage

Create a strong minimalist hero section.

Display:

**STILL FRAME**

Dark / Eerie / Atmospheric Ambient Music

A short introduction such as:

“Dark ambient music inspired by forgotten places, strange worlds and video game atmospheres.”

Include two primary actions:

* **Explore Music**
* **YouTube**

The hero could contain a subtle background image/video-like visual treatment inspired by an old game environment, foggy forest, abandoned corridor or CRT footage.

The visual should remain subtle so the typography and music catalogue remain the focus.

### Music Library

Create a prominent section called:

**Music Archive**

Show music as a collection of minimal cards.

Each track should include:

* Track title
* Short description or mood
* Category/tag
* Duration
* Small play button
* A waveform or very subtle visual audio indicator
* Link to the full YouTube video

Example tracks:

“somewhere between heaven and hell...”
“the woods feel different tonight...”
“a world beyond reality”
“why is someone standing there...”

Include filtering options such as:

**All / Dark / Horror / Game Inspired / Silent Hill / Zelda / Other**

The library should feel more like an atmospheric archive than a commercial music store.

### Audio Player

Design a simple persistent audio player.

It should contain:

* Track title
* Play/pause
* Progress bar
* Current time / duration
* Volume
* Minimal visual feedback when audio is playing

The player should remain visually unobtrusive.

The idea is that visitors can listen to short previews directly on the website.

### Featured Track

Include a larger featured-track section with artwork/background imagery, title, short description and an audio preview.

Example:

**somewhere between heaven and hell...**

“An eerie ambient track somewhere between darkness and serenity.”

Include:

* Play preview
* Listen on YouTube

### Licensing

Create a dedicated section called:

**Use My Music**

Explain that Still Frame music can be licensed for use in games, videos and other creative projects.

Keep the copy simple and professional.

Example:

“Want to use Still Frame music in your game or project? Non-exclusive licensing is available. Get in touch to discuss usage and licensing.”

Include a clear:

**Contact for licensing**

button.

Do not make this look like a large commercial music marketplace. It should feel like an independent creator offering music directly to other creators.

### About

Create a small About section.

Include:

**About Still Frame**

“All music is produced by me in Ableton Live. I'm still learning, experimenting, and exploring new sounds, so expect new moods as I grow.”

Also mention:

**No AI involved in the production of my tracks.**

Keep this section personal and understated.

### YouTube

Include a section linking to the Still Frame YouTube channel.

Show several recent videos in a clean grid with:

* Thumbnail
* Title
* Category
* Duration

Add a button:

**Visit YouTube**

### Footer

Minimal footer containing:

Still Frame

Dark / Eerie / Atmospheric Ambient Music

Links:

* YouTube
* Contact
* Licensing
* Buy Me a Coffee

Include a small copyright notice.

### Overall UX

The website should be:

* Fully responsive
* Desktop-first but excellent on mobile
* Fast and minimal
* Easy to navigate
* Accessible
* Focused on music rather than excessive visual effects

Use subtle animations and transitions, but avoid excessive motion.

Potential effects:

* Very subtle CRT/noise texture
* Slow-moving background imagery
* Gentle fade-ins
* Subtle hover effects
* Minimal waveform animation while audio is playing

The design should feel like a **digital archive from a mysterious game world**.

Think:

**“An underground ambient music archive discovered inside an old computer.”**

But keep it modern enough to feel like a professional 2026 website.

The final design should look realistic to implement with **React + TypeScript**.
